Privacy Policy

Plan the Plate — Last updated: October 2, 2026

Plan the Plate ("the App") is a family meal-ordering tool that lets kids place breakfast and lunch orders from a pantry their parent manages. This policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and who can see it.

Information We Collect

The App stores the following information, entered directly by the parent or family members using the App:

  • Names of children/family members using the App (first names or nicknames only)
  • Pantry items and meal preferences
  • Meal orders and order history
  • Optional notes attached to orders
  • A parent PIN, if one is set, to restrict access to parent-only features

The App does not collect or require: email addresses, phone numbers, physical addresses, payment card details, or any government-issued identification.

How Information Is Used

All information entered into the App is used solely to operate the App's core function: letting family members place meal orders and letting the parent view, manage, and fulfill them. We do not use this information for advertising, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to the App's operation.

Where Information Is Stored

Data is stored securely using Supabase, a third-party database provider, and is associated only with the family's individual App installation. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock premium features. Payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; the App itself never receives or stores any payment card information.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for use within a single family, by parents and their own children, under the parent's supervision and control. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what a parent chooses to enter (such as a child's first name) for the purpose of operating the App within that family's own use. The App is not intended for use by children independently of a parent or guardian setting it up.

Data Retention & Deletion

Parents can delete pantry items, orders, and order history directly within the App at any time. To request complete deletion of all data associated with your family's use of the App, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to: support@plantheplate.com
